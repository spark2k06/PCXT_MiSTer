Local processing

Your files are read by this page and never uploaded. The one-click builds for the IBM 5160, Juko ST, PCjr and EGA only download files, from a pinned revision of the 86Box ROM archive.

What is checked

The built-in images and the EGA ROM are compared byte for byte, by SHA-256, with the images tested on the core. Every downloaded chip is checked by SHA-256 before it is used, and your own dumps are checked for size and reset vector; the SHA-256 of the result is shown.

Missing BIOS

Without the BIOS of the selected machine, or the EGA BIOS while EGA is selected, the core keeps the splash on screen and the OSD names the missing slot.
